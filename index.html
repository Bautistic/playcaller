How to Play

The Idea

An American coach, chasing a career in Europe. The Playcaller is a roguelite: what you earn each run carries over, making your next team a little stronger.

A Match

Every match is 3 drives (own 25, 50, and opp 25). The core of the game is playcalling: read the down & distance and the defense's tendencies, then pick the play that beats what they're likely to do.

A Season

Between matches, spend the Practice Points you've earned at the Training Facilities to sharpen your team for the next one. Season format depends on the championship you pick.

Growing Your Team

Between seasons, spend the XP (Experience Points) and RP (Reputation Points) you've earned in the Front Office: upgrade yourself, upgrade your staff, or recruit Key Players.
